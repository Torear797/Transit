Transtit (“the App”) is a diary application with local analytics, developed by Artem Klych (“Developer”, “we”, “us”). The App does not require an account, has no backend server, and stores your diary entries exclusively on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the App, where it is stored, and how you can control it.

1. Summary

  • We do not collect the content you create. We have no access to your diary entries, dates, tags, or any other information you enter into the App. This data remains in the App’s local storage on your device.
  • We use a limited analytics SDK (AppMetrica) to understand technical usage. AppMetrica processes non-personalised technical data that cannot be used to identify you. You can disable analytics collection at any time (see Section 3).
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising. The only third-party service integrated is AppMetrica, which acts as a data processor on our behalf.
  • The App has no backend. It does not send your diary content to any server controlled by us.

2. Data You Create and Where It Is Stored

The App processes data that you voluntarily create:

  • Diary entry texts;
  • Dates, timestamps, tags, or categories (if you choose to use them);
  • App settings (e.g., display preferences).

All such data is stored exclusively in the local container (sandbox) of the App on your iPhone or iPad. The Developer has no technical means to access, copy, or view this content.

3. Analytics (AppMetrica)

We use AppMetrica, an analytics service provided by Yandex, to collect aggregated, non-personalised technical information about how the App is used. AppMetrica helps us understand app performance and improve the user experience.

What AppMetrica may collect (by default)

  • Device information: OS version, device model, screen resolution;
  • App usage data: launches, taps, session duration, in-app events;
  • Identifiers: a device-level identifier (such as IDFA) only if you have granted tracking permission via the App Tracking Transparency framework; otherwise, the SDK operates without tracking identifiers;
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs, performance metrics.

AppMetrica does not collect the content of your diary entries. According to Yandex, all data processed by AppMetrica is non-personalised and cannot be used to identify an individual user.

How to disable analytics

You can disable the collection of analytics data at any time from the App’s settings (e.g., a “Privacy” or “Analytics” toggle). When disabled, AppMetrica stops sending statistics from your device.

For more information about how Yandex processes data, please refer to the Yandex Privacy Policy and the AppMetrica Data Processing Agreement.

4. Backup and Export

Local backup

The App allows you to create a backup file. This file is generated and saved entirely on your device (or in a location you choose via the iOS file picker). We do not upload the backup to any server.

PDF export

You can export diary entries as a PDF file. The exported file is saved locally on your device or shared through the iOS share sheet at your discretion. The Developer does not receive a copy of the exported file.

iCloud

The App does not use iCloud or any cloud synchronisation. Your data is not automatically backed up to iCloud by the App.

5. Permissions

The App does not request access to sensitive iOS permissions such as notifications, location, contacts, photos, or biometric authentication (Face ID / Touch ID). If future versions require any permission, it will be requested only when necessary for a specific feature, and you will be able to revoke it in iOS Settings.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any account information.
  • We do not access your diary content.
  • We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.
  • We do not use analytics data for advertising or cross-app tracking (unless you explicitly grant tracking permission, in which case AppMetrica may use IDFA as described in Section 3).

7. Security

Because your diary content is stored locally, its protection relies on iOS security mechanisms (app sandboxing, file-system encryption when a passcode is enabled). We recommend using a device passcode and keeping iOS up to date.

8. Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:

  • To delete all diary entries and settings, simply delete the App from your device. This removes the App’s local data.
  • Backup files and exported PDFs that you have saved outside the App (e.g., in Files or shared to another app) are not deleted automatically. You can delete them manually using the respective app or iOS Files.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your jurisdiction). Because the App does not collect personal information and does not require an account, it does not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, you can delete the App from the device.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the “Last updated” date at the top will indicate when the policy was revised. If changes are material, we will endeavour to notify you through the App Store release notes.
